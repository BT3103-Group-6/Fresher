# Consent form: Fresher usability test

**Project:** Fresher, a barber booking app built as a student project for BT3103 (Group 6).

**What we are asking you to do:** try three short tasks on the app (find a barbershop, book a slot, cancel or move the booking) on a laptop, in a phone-sized window. It takes about 15 minutes. We are testing the app, not you.

**Taking part is your choice.**
- You do not have to take part.
- You can stop at any time, for any reason, without explaining why.

**What we record.**
- We record only a participant number (for example P03), not your name, phone number or email.
- We note whether each task was completed, how long it took, and short notes on what was easy or hard.
- There is no audio or video recording, and no screenshots of you.
- In the app, please use a made-up name and the test phone number we give you. The app runs on a test system that is wiped at the end of the testing day.

**How the results are used.**
- The results are anonymised.
- They are used only for the BT3103 project report.

**Questions?** Ask the person running the session at any time.
